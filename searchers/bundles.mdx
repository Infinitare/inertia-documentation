title: Submit bundles
description: Bid tips to place your transactions directly behind a streamed bundle.
---

`SubmitBundles` is a bidirectional stream. You send bids, and Inertia answers every bid with a `BundleUpdate`. The call needs the `intake` permission.

## Open the stream first

Inertia holds a streamed bundle for bidding when it matches your orderflow subscription while your bid stream is open. Open `SubmitBundles` before you subscribe, and keep it open.

Every held bundle gets its own 50 ms auction. The 50 ms start right before Inertia streams the bundle to you, and your network latency counts against them. Near the end of a [leader window](/searchers/orderflow#leader-windows), the auction closes 10 ms before the window ends, even if the 50 ms are not over yet. Some streamed bundles are not up for auction, and a bid on one of them returns `NOT_HELD`. A bundle with 5 transactions is never up for auction, since the limit of 5 transactions per bundle leaves no room for yours.

## Place a bid

A bid is a `Bundle` message:

- `signature` is the signature of the streamed bundle you bid on.
- `transactions` holds your own signed transactions in the same [wire format](/searchers/protocol#transaction-format) as the stream. Leave out the streamed transactions. Inertia puts them in front of yours.

The streamed bundle and your transactions together may hold at most 5 transactions. You can send several bids in one `Bundles` message.

### Tip

Your tip is the sum of all System Program transfers from your transactions to an Inertia tip account. You pay it only if the bundle lands.

Only transfers to the accounts above count. A transfer to a Jito tip account adds nothing to your bid.

### Minimum tip

Inertia currently requires a tip of at least 45% of the profit you expect from the bundle. If you expect a bundle to make 1 SOL, tip at least 0.45 SOL.

### Checks

Inertia checks every transaction in a bid with the same rules as the validator:

- All signatures verify.
- The transaction uses at most 64 accounts and lists each account once.
- It keeps sysvars and built-in programs read-only.
- Its compute budget instructions are valid.
- It uses a valid durable nonce, or a recent blockhash that the node knows.
- Its address lookup tables exist and are active.
- The bid contains no vote transactions.
- Each transaction in the bid uses its own nonce.

A transaction that already passed through the orderflow fails with `DUPLICATE`. Your own transactions stay usable for a new bid after you were outbid.

## Auction

- The highest tip wins. On equal tips, the earlier bid wins.
- When a higher bid arrives, the previous leader gets `Outbid` with the new tip. Your own earlier bid gets it too.
- A bid that does not beat the current leader gets `Outbid` right away, with the winning tip.
- To raise your bid, sign a new transaction with the higher tip and send it as a new bid.

When the auction closes, Inertia sends the winning bundle to the validator, with the streamed transactions first and yours after them. The validator executes the bundle as a whole or not at all. You receive `Sent` with your tip. 10 ms later, Inertia also forwards the streamed transactions on their own as a fallback.

## Updates

Every `BundleUpdate` carries three fields:

- `signature` is the streamed bundle you bid on.
- `bid_signature` is the signature of the first transaction in your bid.
- `update` is one of `Sent`, `Outbid` or `Rejected`.

| Update | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Sent` | Your bid won, and Inertia sent the bundle to the validator. `tip` is your tip. |
| `Outbid` | Another bid tips more. `tip` is the winning tip. |
| `Rejected` | Inertia dropped your bid. `reason` and `detail` say why. |

Read the updates continuously. When your client falls behind, the node drops updates.

### Rejection reasons

| Reason | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `NOT_HELD` | The bundle is unknown, not up for auction, or its auction already closed. |
| `BUNDLE_SIZE` | The bid has no transactions, or more than 5 together with the streamed bundle. |
| `INVALID` | A transaction does not parse. |
| `UNVERIFIED` | A signature does not verify. |
| `DUPLICATE` | A transaction already passed through the orderflow. |
| `NO_TIP` | No transaction transfers to a tip account. |
| `REJECTED` | A transaction broke a validator rule. `detail` names the rule. |
| `DROPPED` | The bid queue of the node was full. Send the bid again. |
| `UNDELIVERABLE` | Your bid won, but no validator accepted the bundle. |

For `REJECTED`, `detail` holds one of these rules:

| Detail | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `account_count` | The transaction uses more than 64 accounts. |
| `duplicate_account` | The transaction lists an account twice. |
| `writes_reserved` | The transaction writes to a sysvar or a built-in program. |
| `tip_program` | The transaction references the Jito Tip Payment or Tip Distribution program. |
| `blocklisted` | The transaction touches a blocked account. |
| `compute_budget` | A compute budget instruction is invalid or repeated. |
| `precompile` | A precompile instruction is too short for its signatures. |
| `invalid_nonce` | The durable nonce instruction is invalid. |
| `bundle_vote` | The bid contains a vote transaction. |
| `nonce_conflict` | Two transactions use the same nonce. |
| `lookup_table` | An address lookup table is deactivated. |
| `lookup_unresolved` | The node cannot load an address lookup table. |
| `expired_blockhash` | The blockhash expired, or the node does not know it. |
| `tracked_pool` | The transaction touches a pool that Inertia locks at the moment. |
