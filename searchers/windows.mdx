---
title: Submit windows
description: Schedule your own transactions for each slot of an upcoming leader window, and reserve an account for them.
---

`SubmitWindow` schedules your own transactions for one upcoming [leader window](/searchers/leader-windows). You assign them to the 4 slots of the window, and Inertia sends each slot's transactions to the leader when that slot starts. You can also reserve an account for the window, such as a pool that your transactions create. The call needs the `windows` permission.

## Submit

```protobuf
message SlotTransactions {
  repeated bytes transactions = 1;
}

message SubmitWindowRequest {
  uint64 first_slot = 1;
  repeated SlotTransactions slots = 2;
  bytes account = 3;
  bytes signature = 4;
  bool allow_buys = 5;
}
```

- `first_slot` is the `first_slot` of a window from [`GetLeaderWindows`](/searchers/leader-windows). The window must not have started yet.
- `slots` holds up to 4 entries, one per slot of the window in slot order. Leave an entry empty to skip that slot.
- `account`, `signature` and `allow_buys` are optional and [reserve an account](#reserve-an-account).

Each slot takes at most 10 transactions in the same [wire format](/searchers/protocol#transaction-format) as the stream. The request needs at least one transaction.

You can submit up to 10 requests per window. Each request is its own submission with its own tip, checks and update stream. An 11th request fails with `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`. A request with a transaction that you already scheduled in this window fails with `ALREADY_EXISTS`. A submission cannot be changed or cancelled.

Submit each window to the node that listed it. Only validators connected to that node count.

### Tip

Your tip is the sum of all System Program transfers from your transactions to an [Inertia tip account](/searchers/bundles#tip). At least one transaction in every request has to tip.

Inertia currently requires a tip of at least 3.5% of the volume your transactions trade in the window. If they trade 100 SOL, tip at least 3.5 SOL.

Every transaction lands on its own. You pay the tip of each transaction that lands, whatever happens to the others.

### Checks

Inertia checks every transaction when you submit, with the same [rules as a bid](/searchers/bundles#checks). When one transaction fails, the call fails with `INVALID_ARGUMENT` and schedules nothing. The message starts with the [rejection reason](/searchers/bundles#rejection-reasons), followed by the rule for `REJECTED`.

## Reserve an account

Set `account` to the 32-byte address of an account that your transactions use. It can be a pool or any other account. Inertia then rejects every other transaction that passes the account to one of its instructions as `tracked_pool`, in the orderflow and in bids. A transaction that only pays its fee with the account, or only invokes it as a program, still passes. The reservation starts 8 slots before the window and ends with its last slot.

Inertia accepts the account under three conditions:

- One of your transactions references it.
- The account does not exist on chain yet, or you [prove that you hold its key](#reserve-an-existing-account).
- No other searcher has reserved it, and Inertia does not lock it at the moment.

A later submission for the same window can name an account that one of your earlier submissions reserved. The reservation keeps the `allow_buys` of the first submission.

When the account is a pool, set `allow_buys` to let buys from other senders on the pool through. Sells and every other transaction stay rejected.

The reservation covers only the transactions that pass through Inertia.

### Reserve an existing account

To reserve an account that already exists on chain, prove that you hold its key. Sign the string `inertia-window:` followed by the `first_slot` of your request in decimal, for example `inertia-window:444081652`.

Sign the UTF-8 bytes of that string with the account's private key, using ed25519 and no off-chain message header. Put the 64-byte signature in `signature` of your request.

With a `signature`, Inertia skips the check that the account is new. A signature that does not match the account and the `first_slot` fails the call with `PERMISSION_DENIED`.

Each signature works for one window. Sign again for every window you submit.

## Sending

About 10 ms after a slot of the window starts, Inertia sends that slot's transactions to the leader. It sends them one by one in your order, and the validator keeps that order. When you have several submissions in a window, Inertia sends them in the order they arrived. When the node missed the start of a slot, it sends that slot's transactions together with the next slot's, ahead of them.

Right before it sends a transaction, Inertia checks it against the locked pools and the reserved accounts again. A transaction that touches a pool Inertia locks, or an account another searcher reserved, gets `Rejected` with `tracked_pool`. Accounts that your own submissions reserved for this window pass.

## Updates

The call returns a stream of [`BundleUpdate`](/searchers/bundles#updates) messages, one stream per submission. `bid_signature` is the signature of the transaction the update is about. `signature` stays empty.

| Update | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Sent` | Inertia sent the transaction to the leader. `tip` is the tip of this transaction, and `rank` is 0. |
| `Landed` | The validator executed the transaction and put it in its block. `slot` is the slot of that block. |
| `Dropped` | The validator executed the transaction and left it out of its block, because it failed. The fields match a [dropped bundle](/searchers/bundles#outcome). |
| `Rejected` | Inertia did not send the transaction. `reason` is `REJECTED` with the detail `tracked_pool`, or `UNDELIVERABLE`. |

`UNDELIVERABLE` means that the leader was not connected when the slot started, or that the window passed before Inertia could send the transaction.

`Landed` and `Dropped` only follow `Sent`. A transaction that the validator never executed gets neither.

The stream ends about 10 slots after the last slot of the window. It also ends when Inertia revokes the `windows` permission. Inertia then drops the transactions it has not sent yet and releases the account.

## Errors

| Status | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| `PERMISSION_DENIED` | The key lacks the `windows` permission, or `signature` does not match the account and the `first_slot`. |
| `INVALID_ARGUMENT` | No transactions, more than 4 slots, more than 10 transactions in a slot, a transaction that failed a check, an account that is not 32 bytes long, or an account that none of your transactions references. |
| `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `first_slot` is not the first slot of an upcoming window on this node, or the account already exists and the request has no `signature`. |
| `ALREADY_EXISTS` | One of the transactions is already scheduled in this window, or the account is already reserved or locked. |
| `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` | The window already has 10 of your submissions, or the node has too many submissions to check. In the second case, send the request again. |
| `UNAVAILABLE` | The node could not check the transactions or the account. Send the request again. |
